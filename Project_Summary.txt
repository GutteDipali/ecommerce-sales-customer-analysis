E-COMMERCE SALES & CUSTOMER ANALYSIS

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analysis is a data analytics and business intelligence project developed using Power BI. The project analyzes e-commerce sales and customer data to identify sales trends, customer distribution, product performance, regional performance, payment methods, and business insights.

2. OBJECTIVE

The objective of this project is to transform e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights using an interactive Power BI dashboard and support data-driven business decisions.

3. TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES

- Power BI
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- Jira

4. KEY ANALYSIS AREAS

- Overall sales performance
- Customer analysis
- Product performance
- Category analysis
- Regional sales
- Payment method analysis
- Sales trends over time
- Product quantity analysis
- Business insights

5. KEY DAX MEASURES

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

6. KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS

- Electronics is the highest-selling category.
- South is the highest-performing region.
- Laptop is the top-selling product.
- Credit Card is the most-used payment method.
- September is the best-performing sales month.

7. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

- Promote high-performing products.
- Plan inventory based on product demand.
- Focus marketing activities on regional performance.
- Use seasonal campaigns during strong sales periods.
- Monitor payment-method preferences.

8. PROJECT MANAGEMENT

Jira was used for project planning, activity tracking, documentation, and project completion management.

9. PROJECT OUTCOME

The project demonstrates the use of Power BI and DAX to convert raw e-commerce data into interactive dashboards and actionable business insights.

10. FUTURE SCOPE

- Add more advanced customer segmentation.
- Implement predictive sales analysis.
- Add automated dashboard refresh.
- Integrate additional business KPIs.
