E-COMMERCE SALES & CUSTOMER ANALYSIS
BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. TOP CATEGORY

Electronics is the highest-selling product category.

Business implication:
The business can maintain sufficient inventory of high-performing Electronics products and use targeted promotions to support sales.

2. TOP REGION

South is the highest-performing sales region.

Business implication:
The business can study successful strategies in the South region and use suitable approaches in other regions.

3. TOP PRODUCT

Laptop is the top-selling product.

Business implication:
Laptops can be included in promotional campaigns and inventory planning based on customer demand.

4. PAYMENT METHOD

Credit Card is the most-used payment method.

Business implication:
The business can ensure a smooth and reliable credit-card payment experience while continuing to support other payment options.

5. BEST MONTH

September is the best-performing sales month.

Business implication:
The business can plan marketing campaigns, inventory, and promotional activities around periods with strong sales performance.

6. OVERALL BUSINESS INSIGHT

The Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, customers, products, regions, payment methods, and time-based trends.

The analysis helps identify high-performing categories, regions, products, payment methods, and sales periods that can support business planning and decision-making.
