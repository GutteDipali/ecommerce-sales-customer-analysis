E-COMMERCE SALES & CUSTOMER ANALYSIS
DAX MEASURES

1. TOTAL SALES

Total Sales represents the total sales/revenue generated from the e-commerce transactions.

Measure:
Total Sales = SUM('ecommerce_sales'[Sales])


2. TOTAL ORDERS

Total Orders represents the total number of orders in the dataset.

Measure:
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT('ecommerce_sales'[Order_ID])


3. TOTAL CUSTOMERS

Total Customers represents the number of unique customers.

Measure:
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT('ecommerce_sales'[Customer_ID])


4. TOTAL QUANTITY

Total Quantity represents the total quantity of products sold.

Measure:
Total Quantity = SUM('ecommerce_sales'[Quantity])


5. AVERAGE ORDER VALUE

Average Order Value represents the average sales value per order.

Measure:
Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders])
